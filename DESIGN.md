# Handoff design direction

Identity: a small desktop utility for moving files between nearby devices.

Personality: quiet, useful, slightly nostalgic. The interface borrows the clear framing of old desktop transfer dialogs and the stillness of liminal spaces.

Palette: charcoal surroundings, warm gray work surface, muted ink, and one pale chartreuse accent for the active connection.

Typography: system sans for instructions and filenames; system monospace for addresses, sizes, and transfer details.

Mood: a quiet room with one clear route between devices. The dashed transfer line marks that route and also explains the product.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

Reading this as: a local file transfer utility for people moving files between their own devices, in a quiet old desktop interface style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
